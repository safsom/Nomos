# Source

All files here are from 殆知閣古代文獻藏書 (Daizhige), mirrored at
https://github.com/garychowcmu/daizhigev20 (commit fetched 2026-09-22).

The underlying works are long out of copyright -- the latest, 刑案匯覽三編,
dates to 1886. The *digitisation* carries no stated licence. We record that
rather than asserting terms the compiler does not give. See DATA_LICENSES.md.

Texts are in simplified characters, as Daizhige distributes them.
